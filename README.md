Wellness Arcade 🌿🎮
Role: Frontend Developer
Wellness Arcade is a web app designed to make self-care fun and manageable. In fast-paced environments, even basic wellness tasks—like drinking water or brushing teeth—can become overwhelming, especially for those facing mental health challenges. This app turns daily routines into a gamified, lighthearted experience, helping users build healthy habits without stress or shame.
Inspiration. Taking care of one’s wellbeing can be difficult in a busy life. Daily self-care tasks often get neglected, leading to feelings of guilt or shame.
Wellness Arcade was created to empower users to manage their routines in a fun and motivating way. By gamifying self-care, the app encourages consistent habits while making the process enjoyable rather than burdensome.
Features: 
Health tracking: Log daily water intake, teeth brushing, and other essential self-care tasks.
Mindfulness exercises: Guided deep breathing, memory-based mini-games, and exploring emotional responses.
Daily affirmations: Build personalized affirmations from a curated word bank.
Gamified interface: Turns routine tasks into a fun, interactive experience.
How We Built It:
Started by identifying core tasks: water intake and teeth brushing.
Added awareness exercises for mindfulness and calm.
Focused on gamification to make healthy behaviors rewarding.
Used trial and error, brainstorming, and collaboration to refine features and UI.
Challenges:
Limited experience developing a feature-rich web app under tight timelines.
Difficulty with API integration and deployment, leading to numerous crashes.
Navigated challenges through team coordination, research, and persistence, ultimately deploying the app with all original features intact.
Future Improvements:
Accessibility: Multi-language support, voice guidance, and features for users with disabilities.
Educational content: Mental health resources and habit-building research for sustainable lifestyle improvements.
Enhanced gamification: Additional activities to reinforce daily wellness habits.
